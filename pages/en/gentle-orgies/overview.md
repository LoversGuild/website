# Open arms, open hearts

For us, sex is a bodily expression of love.
It is a miraculous gift from life: enchantingly beautiful, at times even scary in its power.
We don't want to be ashamed of, or to conceal, this gift, rather, we want to celebrate it from the bottom of our hearts.

In Gentle Orgies we explore intimacy and lovemaking together in a shared space.
With a mix of free play and guided exercises, we focus on our own **bodily, emotional and spiritual experience of sex**.
We share our pure and innocent desire, our beautiful naked bodies, our untamed playfulness, our joy and painful emotions, and our own ways to make love.
We welcome all kinds of consensual, conscious and gentle sexual expression.
Even in our wildest play, we want to be able to look each other in the eyes and see, beyond desire, a clear sense of love and care.

All play and participation in the exercises in Gentle orgies is completely voluntary.
You decide in every moment how—and with whom—you want to interact.

By **love**, we do not mean the feelings of falling in love or attachment between people.
For us, love is a state of unconditional acceptance and a feeling that we are all significant to each other at the moment of our meeting.

In addition to making love, we witness each other’s playing, eat, drink tea, talk and sleep together.
Our orgies are sober.

# Deepening one's self-knowledge

Gentle Orgies are both a journey into yourself and a journey into connection with others.  
When we get to know one another, we also get to know ourselves deeply: how we communicate, our desires and our insecurities.

Our ability to enjoy sex is largely based on how open and willing to receive we are.
This has as much to do with our bodies as our minds.
With accepting, conscious presence, we are able to develop our sensitivity and our ability to give in to pleasure.

In the beginning part of our orgies we do guided exercises that are bodily and meditative meetings with ourselves and one another.
During these exercises we explore and express our boundaries, feelings, desires and fears, we challenge ourselves to face our sore points, we celebrate the connections with one another and open up our bodies for lovemaking.
Our favourite practice methods include breathing, souding, movement, touch, talking, silence, focusing and dance.

We hope that in Gentle orgies, everyone learns something new to take home with them to enrich their own and their loved ones’ lives. 

# Intimacy and genuine meetings

We aim to make our gatherings very intimate.
A part of this intimacy is a small amount of participants.
In a group of about 14 to 18 people it is easy for everyone to remember each other’s names and boundaries, and it is possible to create a personal connection with everyone present.

When we make love, we do not selfishly focus on satisfying our own needs, but also on wholly paying attention to each and every person we meet.
We listen to the desires and wishes of our lovers, we read their body language and we try to find mutual pleasure with them. 

# Naturalness

You can come to the orgies as your genuine self.
You need not make yourself overly presentable, put on makeup, trim your body hair or dress in any particular way.

Menstruation is not a problem for us.
We appreciate fertility and the menstrual cycle deeply, and we consider it an honour that many join our orgies during such a sensitive part of the cycle.

Our orgies do not have background music.
The sounds of pleasure, lovemaking and breathing ring far more beautiful than any instrument. 
Our view of most sex toys is also fairly distrustful.
We believe that the loving touch of many partners offers a deeper satisfaction than mechanical toys. 

# Being accepted and seen

In Gentle orgies, you can open up your heart as well as your legs.
All feelings and their expression is a part of lovemaking.
Sometimes revealing your bodily and mental nakedness can feel very painful.

In Gentle orgies, everyone can trust that all feelings in themselves are welcome and accepted.
In the world that we live in, there are very few situations where we can feel ourselves seen, accepted and loved this deeply.
Such experiences can be very healing.

Most of us have been conditioned since childhood to be ashamed of our body and sexuality.
It is very rewarding to let go of this shame, yet it is not an easy task for most.

In our orgies, no one has a wrong kind of body or a wrong way to express their pleasure with their voice.
We want to make love with you in particular.

Nervousness and shyness are also a part of orgies.
We want to encourage everyone to express and fulfil their wishes.
However, we do not believe that anyone needs to get rid of their nervousness in a hurry.
Our bodies and minds will relax when they are ready for it.

During an orgy, organizers are always ready to personally support everyone.
No one is left alone after the orgy either, you can always contact the organizers should you want to discuss your experiences.
Every participant should also be prepared to be present to their lovemaking partners—not just during moments of pleasure, but pain, too.

# Boundaries

For all of our sexual interactions to be fully consensual, we must be fully aware of both our own and others’ boundaries.
Boundaries must be first recognized, then expressed.

When you try to find out your own boundaries, it is important to listen to your body and mind.
Both continuously signal to us about what we want, what we don’t want, what fascinates us, what makes us nervous and what we fear.
Our society that shackles and does not appreciate sexuality does not encourage us to listen to or express our boundaries.
That is why these skills must be learned, often as adults. 

For us, paying attention to our partners’ verbal and bodily communication is the starting point for all consent, and therefore sexual interaction too.
“No” always means no, unequivocally and immediately!
We do not stretch one another’s boundaries nor do we try to make anyone stretch their boundaries for us.
We also practice the act of recognizing and expressing boundaries in every orgy. 

# Privacy

Respecting one another’s privacy is also a vital part of orgies.
Though the general atmosphere has become more accepting lately, stigmatization is still a real threat to many.
For many, sex is a private matter and we want to respect everyone having the possibility to keep their adventures only between them and their partners.

Therefore the knowledge of identities of the participants shall remain to only those who attended the orgy.
Naturally, everyone can freely share about their own experiences at the orgy, but nobody may reveal anyone’s identity to outsiders.
Cameras and phones are not to be taken out during orgies.

# Safety

Safety is born from trust, respecting one another and open communication.

Trust and openness is born from getting to know one another.
At the beginning of every orgy, we introduce ourselves, tell others about ourselves and our wishes, our boundaries, our protection principles and our STI status.
Everybody needs to get tested for sexually transmitted infections before every orgy.

To ensure a safe atmosphere, we always start the orgies together.
Anyone is free to leave whenever they wish to.
