**The Lovers’ Guild** is a Finnish grassroots community for conscious, loving sexuality.
We organise small events where sexuality, intimacy, spirituality and mindful presence meet—with clear boundaries and deep respect for everyone present.
Not all our events contain sex.

Our vision is a world where sexuality is honoured and celebrated consciously, freely and openly, without shame or fear.
Our core events are called **[Gentle Orgies][orgy]**.
They are weekend‑long, sober, carefully held mini-retreats for people who want to explore sexuality as a path of self‑knowledge, deep connection, deep pleasure and spiritual growth in a safe space.

Our community attracts:

- people of all genders who are interested in **mindfulness, neotantra, yoga, meditation, bodywork or other inner work**, and
- people for whom sex is **emotionally meaningful way to connect with others**, not just physical release.

We are **not** a casual sex club or a place to “just try an orgy”.
Our events are intimate, emotionally deep, and require readiness to communicate honestly, listen to your own boundaries and respect those of others.

At the Lovers’ Guild we:

- organise **Gentle Orgies**—mini-retreats for conscious sexual exploration in a loving, safe, sober space,
- organise workshops with varying themes,
- develop **guides, exercises and communication materials** for conscious sexuality events,
- offer **support and consultation** to people who want to organise similar spaces in their own communities, and
- share our **materials and open‑source tools** on this website and [our GitHub][github].

On these pages you can explore our philosophy, read about our community culture, our rules, our responsibility principles, and how to apply to join.
