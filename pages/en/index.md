**The Lovers’ Guild** is a Finnish grassroots community for conscious, loving sexuality.
Our vision is a world where sexuality is honoured and celebrated consciously, freely and openly, without shame or fear.

We organise events where sexuality, mindful presence, emotional connection and authentic relating meet.
In some of our events, participants make love together, while in others sexuality is explored differently.
All sexual interaction in our events is always based on consent and on respecting everyone’s boundaries.

Our core activity is hosting retreats of conscious sexual exploration called [**Gentle Orgies**][orgy]—weekend‑long, sober and carefully held.
They are for people who want to explore sexuality as a path of self‑knowledge, intimate connection, deep pleasure and spiritual growth in a safe space.

Our community attracts people of all genders:

- who are interested in **mindfulness, neotantra, yoga, meditation, bodywork or other inner work**,
- for whom sex is **emotionally meaningful way to connect with others**, not just physical release, and
- who have good emotional and interaction skills, and the ability to listen and respect their own and others’ boundaries.

We are **not** a sex club or a place to “just try an orgy.”  
We are not a cult, nor do we bow to gurus: for us, spirituality doesn’t mean religiosity, but conscious presence in all its richness.

At the Lovers’ Guild we:

- organise **Gentle Orgies**—retreats for loving conscious sexuality,
- organise workshops with varying themes,
- develop **guides, exercises and communication materials** for conscious sexuality events,
- offer **support and consultation** to people who want to organise similar spaces in their own communities, and
- share our **materials and open‑source tools** on this website and [our GitHub][github].

On these pages you can explore our philosophy, read about our community culture, our rules, our responsibility principles, and how to apply to join.
